# Overview — for People Who Weren't There

A one-screen overview for readers who didn't attend and want to know what was discussed and what came out of it: colleagues, adjacent teams, stakeholders, or the operator months later.

It is a separate output from the full notes. It is derived from the finished notes, never written straight from the transcript, so the two never disagree. It is not the leadership view. That one goes through `delivery-communication` and removes names and assignments. The overview keeps owners, because readers need to know whom to ask.

All Core and Neutrality rules in SKILL.md apply, and so do the formatting rules (SKILL.md, Structure): one point per line, a bold label never carries prose, and fragments instead of sentences. The structure follows the same layers as the notes, compressed. A reader who stops after any section still has the most important information up to that point.

---

## Template

```markdown
# [Meeting Name] — Overview — [YYYY-MM-DD]

[Name] ([Role]) · [Name] ([Role])
Does this affect you? [Team] · [Team] · [Product] / Attendees only

## Quick Recap
**[The question or problem the meeting was about]**
- **[Key topic]:** [one short fragment]
- **[Key topic]:** [one short fragment]

## Decisions
- **[What was decided]**: [reason, one clause] ([D-ID])

## Still Open
- **[What is unresolved]**: [who decides, with whom] ([D-ID if logged])

## What Happens Next
- **[Owner]**
  - [Action] · due [YYYY-MM-DD / No date set]

Full notes: [path or link]
```

---

## Section notes

- **Length** — one screen at most. Every decision, open item, and next step from the notes still appears.
- **Quick Recap, Decisions, Still Open** — copied from the notes, word for word. The overview is the top of the notes, without the Discussion Summary.
- **Does this affect you?** — the line that saves a non-attendee from reading further.
- **What Happens Next** — all next steps from the notes, grouped under bold owner labels, one action per line.
- **Full notes** — always included. If there's no link or path yet, write "Full notes: to be published."
- **Leave out** — the Discussion Summary, Carried Over, Not Reached, and Proposed Agenda.

## Final checks (overview)

1. Could someone who wasn't there read only the header and Quick Recap and know whether to keep reading?
2. Does every product decision and next step in the notes appear here, with the same IDs, owners, and dates?
3. Is there anything here that isn't in the full notes? Remove it.
